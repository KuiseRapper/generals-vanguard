## Generals Vanguard 0.5.11

The Russia faction now starts and fights with its own models.

- **The Sapery's dozer** is RotR's dozer, with its construction and damaged animations.
- **The Motostrelki** and the **Kornet team** are RotR's conscript and RPG-conscript models (skinned,
  with their own animations).
- **Verified in a self-driving skirmish** (no mouse or keyboard): the scenario spawned a T-72
  section, two Mstas, BMP-3s, a Tunguska, the supply truck, a MiG-25, six Motostrelki and three
  Kornet teams around the base; the dozer, all of them and the RotR command bunker rendered
  together. The same run also captured the Russia load screen (the star card and the feature line).

Still placeholders: the remaining infantry, the other aircraft, the superweapon effect.

Version 0.5.11 game and launcher.

## macOS, and the launcher

- `VanguardLauncher-mac.zip` and `VanguardGame-mac.zip` are attached again. The launcher's manifest is
  now `releases/latest/download/launcher-mac.json`, so **Check for updates** finds new releases
  instead of the pinned 0.5.3 one, and the launcher replaces itself from the download rather than
  asking you to drag it out of a disk image.
- The launcher has a **Mods** sheet: ShockWave 1.201 and Rise of the Reds 1.8 are listed with
  checkboxes, **Update mods** installs a checked mod from the matching archive in `~/Downloads` (or
  opens its ModDB page when there is none) and removes an unchecked one, with a progress bar.
  Custom `.zip`/`.big` files can be installed and uninstalled too.

## Generals Vanguard 0.5.15

The AI remembers which road gained nothing and takes another, a beaten computer stops building, and
an expansion that finds no room asks the next dock. The combined mod's ECA tank path runs.

### AI

- **"AI wave memory", a skirmish check box, on by default.** Every wave records its lane, its power
  and the AI's win chance at the time; the next wave down that lane (or ninety seconds later) is
  compared. A lane that gained less than three points of chance is barred for two minutes, and the
  lane vote - or Easy/Medium's fallback - takes the next quietest road. Nothing touches production or
  the economy: the worst case is that the army alternates lanes.
  `-nowavememory` turns it off for batches and is the A/B control. The setting travels in the game
  options string, so a replay or a LAN game carries it.
- **A beaten computer stops building.** An AI that has had a factory, barracks or airfield, has none
  left and is not winning places nothing new: no power, no defenses, no factories, no expansions, no
  tunnels, no rebuild. Its army keeps fighting and what still stands is a finite list - the endgame
  sprawl you reported.
- **An expansion asks another dock.** When the dock it picked has nowhere legal to build, the AI now
  tries the next one (up to three) instead of re-asking the same dock and never expanding.
- **The ravine work is in**, after your play-test: unreachable lanes are kept out of the attack lane
  vote, Easy and Medium go around them, the split wave stays off a bad second road, and a unit is no
  longer handed a route to the rim of a ravine.

### Combined mod

- **The Leopard path runs.** The probe was missing the Leopard's prerequisite - a Solar Reactor, not
  the factory alone - so the queue refused every one of them (`CANMAKE_NO_PREREQ`); the
  Overlord-style draw had nothing to do with it. With the reactor standing, `combined_eca.txt` queues
  3 of 3 Leopards; `combined_eca2.txt` queues 2 of 2 Lynx APCs and 2 of 2 Leopards.

### What was measured, and what was not

- Verified: two 400,000-frame Hard matches on Alpine Assault, seven wave sends, four lane judgements,
  and seed 1 barring lane 0 at frame 29,498 for 120 s.  The wave-memory A/B at four players and 96
  cells (six matched seeds) gave byte-identical matches before the strike count was tuned, so **no
  win-rate delta is claimed** yet.
- Not verified: the endgame rule needs a match where an AI actually loses every production building -
  that is your play-test.  The reveal toggle and the "enemy structures: N" counter are still open.

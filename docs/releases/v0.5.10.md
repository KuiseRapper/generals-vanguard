## Generals Vanguard 0.5.10

The Rise of the Reds unit models, and a way to watch a match without a mouse.

- **Five Russia units are RotR's models now**, with their damage states: the **T-72B** (RotR's Rhino
  hull), the **2S19 Msta**, the **Tunguska**, the **supply truck** and the **MiG-25**. Their
  replacements use the base unit's own draw type (`W3DTankDraw`, `W3DTruckDraw`, `W3DModelDraw`),
  which is what the INI requires; a different type is silently skipped.
- **Verified in a skirmish** on this machine: a self-driving scenario spawned a T-72 section, two
  Mstas, a BMP-3 section, a Tunguska, the supply truck and a MiG-25 around the base, and they
  rendered with the new hulls (screenshot captured through the window, no input).
- **New test harness:** `Data/Scenarios/russia_show.txt` plus the `-scenario` system spawns, moves,
  produces and fires powers on given frames. The whole match can be driven and screenshotted without
  touching the mouse or keyboard, and `-headless` runs the same file for logic checks.

Still placeholders: the infantry, the other aircraft, and the superweapon effect.

Version 0.5.10 game and launcher.

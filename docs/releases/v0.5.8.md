## Generals Vanguard 0.5.8

The Rise of the Reds art pass, finished on the buttons and portraits.

- **Build-button cameos are Russia's.** The nine Russian buildings now use RotR's own cameo art
  (`SRCommandBunker`, `SRCoalPowerPlant`, `SRBarracks`, `SRWarfactory`, `SRAirfield`,
  `SRSupplyCenter`, `SRRadarStation`, `SRWeaponBunker`, `SRTremor`) instead of the borrowed base
  models' cameos. Their large portraits (`..._L`, for the selected-building panel) come with them.
  The cameo sheets (`SNRUserInterface512_003/004/005.tga`) ship in `Art/Textures`.
- The models themselves landed in 0.5.7 (Command Bunker, power plant, barracks, war factory,
  airfield, supply centre, radar, weapon bunker, Tremor).
- Credit and licensing: SWR Productions' Rise of the Reds 1.8 art, not GPL; see
  [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md) and [docs/modding/rotr-1.8.md](../../docs/modding/rotr-1.8.md).
- Still placeholders: units and aircraft, and the superweapon effect.

Version 0.5.8 game and launcher.

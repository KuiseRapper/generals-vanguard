## Generals Vanguard 0.5.5

The Russia-faction polish patch: the loading screen, the build menu and the cameos.

- **The loading screen is Russia's.** It showed the China dragon portrait and the word **"Observing"**
  because a faction with no GeneralPersona fell back to its base side's logo and its `Features` text
  was empty. Russia now has its own load portrait (red star, `RUSSIA`, `Generals Vanguard`) and its
  own feature line, so neither fallback is reached.
- **The build menu reads like the USA dozer's**: power plant first, then barracks, supply centre, war
  factory, airfield, tech, defences, and the Command Bunker where USA keeps its Command Center.
  Superweapon last. The Command Bunker no longer starts the list.
- **Real cameos on the buttons.** The whites and letters were placeholders; every construction button
  now uses the cameo of the model it actually builds (the China/USA art the reskin borrows), so the
  bar shows buildings, tanks and aircraft instead of silhouettes.
- **Russia's own star icon and logo** replace the clipped side icon, and the General's Powers screen
  is wired to the eight Russia sciences (see 0.5.4's notes; this package is the first with them).
- The AI's Russia skill sets buy every power across its five sets.

## Generals Vanguard 0.5.7

**Russia's buildings are Rise of the Reds' buildings.** The faction no longer puts up China's models.

- Nine buildings now use the **Rise of the Reds 1.8** (SWR Productions) Russian models and their own
  textures, with the damaged, snow, night, under-construction and build-up variants that go with
  them: Command Bunker, Thermal Power Plant, Regimental Depot, Mobilization Plant, Air Base, Supply
  Center, Fire Direction Center, Strongpoint and Missile Brigade.
- Verified in a skirmish on this machine: the base builds the circular fenced Russian command bunker
  from RotR instead of the China command centre. The models ship in `Art/W3D` in the macOS app
  overlay and in the Windows package (both staging lists were missing that folder).
- **Credit:** these models and textures are SWR Productions' work from Rise of the Reds 1.8 and are
  **not** GPL; see the third-party section in [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md) and the extraction notes in
  [docs/modding/rotr-1.8.md](../../docs/modding/rotr-1.8.md). The rest of the faction is still ours.
- Still placeholders: the units and aircraft (China/USA models), the build-button cameos, and the
  superweapon effect.

Version 0.5.7 game and launcher.

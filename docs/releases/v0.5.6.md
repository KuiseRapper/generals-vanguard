## Generals Vanguard 0.5.6

The General's Powers screen now works with Russia, and this one was verified by playing.

- **The powers screen is populated.** Playing a skirmish as Russia, building a power plant and a
  supply centre to reach 1 Star General, opening the powers screen: the eight Russia powers are there,
  rank 1 / 3 / 5 rows, with their own icons. Buying **Artillery Preparation** spent the point and the
  tooltip read "Call a scheduled artillery barrage on a point. Three waves once the science is fully
  bought." (0.5.4 showed the rank rows with nothing in them; 0.5.5 had no purchase sets.)
- **The powers now require Russia's own sciences.** This was the blocker behind "I buy it and nothing
  happens": every Russia special power still required another faction's science
  (`SCIENCE_ArtilleryBarrage1`, `SCIENCE_ClusterMines`, `SCIENCE_Frenzy1`, …), so a science bought in
  the Russia powers screen never unlocked its power. Each one now requires the Russia science that
  the powers screen sells.
- **Removed the dead tier links** that pointed the Katyusha, Guards and Repair upgrade OCLs at other
  factions' science names.
- Build order, cameos and the load screen from 0.5.5 are unchanged.

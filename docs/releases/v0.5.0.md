## Generals Vanguard 0.5.0 — the Russia pass

- **Command bar fixed.** One vehicle factory (the Tank Works is folded into the Mobilization Plant), the
  same build order as the other sides, a **Radar** button, the Fire Direction Centre no longer vanishes
  after one is built, and the **Command Doctrine gate is gone** — the powers are bought in **General's
  Powers**, the action buttons sit in the Command Bunker.
- **Unique Russia art.** A 40-icon atlas and distinct side/general/score/load images cover every Russia
  building, unit, power and upgrade; the Russia side uses its red house colour. The `MappedImage`s use
  `RUIcons.tga` and the new menu textures.
- **Superweapon — Siberian Front.** A wide EMP drop with a cold red decal: it disables units and
  defences instead of deleting them with the neutron missile. Its own science, OCL and power.
- **Jet run — MiG Inferno.** Five MiGs, two napalm missiles each, leaving the fire field behind; its own
  science and power.
- **Powers.** The two new powers have their own OCLs/sciences; the rest keep their engine effects but
  get Russia names, icons and the normal science purchase. Unique replacement OCLs for the remaining
  effects are the next pass.
- **Still open:** the spawned 3D models are still reskins of existing China/USA models. Genuinely new
  W3D models need a modelling/export pipeline; the menu art and icons are now ours.

Game and launcher are 0.5.0; the macOS DMG and game app are attached.

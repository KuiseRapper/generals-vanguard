Generals Vanguard
for Command & Conquer: Generals Zero Hour

EA has not endorsed and does not support this product.

This installer installs the Generals Vanguard launcher only. It does not include
Electronic Arts' game data, and it does not include any mod package. You need
your own legitimate copy of Command & Conquer: Generals Zero Hour.

The launcher will look for your Zero Hour folder, repair the per-user install
path when it can, connect the base-game files Zero Hour shares, and then offer
the game and three optional mods. Nothing large is downloaded until you choose
it.
